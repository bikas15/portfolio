How to play

Tap a tile to flip it and its neighbors above, below, left and right. On timed levels, the clock starts with your first move.

Use Arrow keys to move around the board and Enter to flip. Clear a level without hints in the fewest moves to earn three stars.

चाल वा समय सकिनुअघि सबै खाना नीलो बनाउनुहोस्। एउटा खाना थिच्दा त्यो खाना र त्यसको ठीक माथि, तल, बायाँ र दायाँका खानाको रङ बदलिन्छ। कुनामा छोएका खानाको रङ बदलिँदैन।
